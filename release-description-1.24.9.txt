## ✨ PlazCode 1.24.9

More reliable AI chats, simpler skills, complete tool results and a cleaner desktop.

### 🛠️ More reliable chats

- **Startup and commands:** Notion draft checks and DeepSeek handshakes are improved. Stalled waits end safely without repeating commands.
- **Answer boxes:** you can type responses to the AI’s questions.

### 🧩 Skills and Ultracode

- **Simpler skills:** name, write and save; import JSON, text or Markdown; remove skills you no longer need.
- **UltraGUI:** relevant UI guidance is included with Ultracode, loaded only when needed.

### 🎨 Cleaner desktop

- **Builders removed:** ask your AI to make UI, or use Blender for models.
- **Clearer tasks:** names describe the AI’s goal; continue keeps the plan. Spacing and Settings alignment are improved.
- **Screenshots:** click to enlarge, copy or use as feedback. Unopened captures disappear after 10 seconds.

### ⚙️ Large scripts and Blender

- **Complete results:** customize character limits and read large results in pages. Default task time limits are off.
- **Blender fixes:** corrected primitives, detailed mesh exports and screenshot reads.

### 🧪 Checks

- Platform/browser regressions and real Blender tests passed. Live AI chats, Studio imports and AI response speed were not verified.

### 📥 How to update

- Open PlazCode, or choose **Updates → Update now**.
- Manual downloads: **PlazCode-1.24.9.zip** for Windows, **PlazCode-macOS-1.24.9.zip** for Mac, or **PlazCode-Firefox-1.24.9.zip** for Firefox.
- Reload your extension and refresh your AI tabs. Firefox users: reload the temporary add-on after restarting Firefox.
- Your settings, memory and connections are kept.

Test details are included in VALIDATION.txt.
