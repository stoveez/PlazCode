## ✨ PlazCode 1.24.15

ClickUp support, unified Ultra effort, recoverable Skills and updated donations.

### ✨ New features

- Use PlazCode tools in ClickUp Brain and existing AI-agent chats.
- Ultra effort combines hidden UltraCode/UltraGUI methods with selected-only UltraLab enhancements and animated labels.
- Restore Deleted Skills or permanently remove them, including a separate trash Delete All.
- Donation links are updated and clearly labelled, with a new desktop Donations page.

### 🛠️ Fixes

- Bounded, confirmed ClickUp startup and a chatbox-aligned toolbar with usable Ask/Agents tabs.
- Condo Lock removed across supported sites; unrelated permissions remain.

### 🧪 Checks

- Approved after the user tested build 3 in ClickUp; platform/browser and packaged Mac checks passed.
- Older hard-deleted skills are not recoverable. Windows unsigned; Mac not notarized.

### 📥 How to update

- Open PlazCode, or choose **Updates → Update now**.
- Manual downloads: **PlazCode-1.24.15.zip** for Windows, **PlazCode-macOS-1.24.15.zip** for Mac, or **PlazCode-Firefox-1.24.15.zip** for Firefox.
- Reload your extension and refresh your AI tabs. Firefox users: reload the temporary add-on after restarting Firefox.
- Your settings, memory and connections are kept.
