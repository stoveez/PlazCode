## ✨ PlazCode 1.24.5

A focused DeepSeek thinking mode, less repeated work and more reliable Notion startup.

- **13 skills:** a focused Roblox method, including safer data saving, UI and API checks. Replies use your language.

### ⚡ Smoother Notion startup

- **Shorter first message:** startup uses a small handshake. Full task instructions follow in the existing tool-list reply.
- **Firefox fix:** startup pastes now carry the complete message.
- **Error recovery:** failed startup releases the chat lock. PlazCode only says it is ready after the handshake succeeds.

### ✅ Checked before release

- Chromium, Firefox and platform regression checks, plus the macOS desktop lifetime check. Live signed-in account behavior remains unverified.

### 📥 How to update

- Open PlazCode, or choose **Updates → Update now**.
- Manual downloads: **PlazCode-1.24.5.zip** for Windows, **PlazCode-macOS-1.24.5.zip** for Mac, or **PlazCode-Firefox-1.24.5.zip** for Firefox.
- Reload your extension and refresh your AI tabs. Firefox users: reload the temporary add-on after restarting Firefox.
- Your settings, memory and connections are kept.
