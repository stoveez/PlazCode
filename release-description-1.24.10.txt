## ✨ PlazCode 1.24.10

Start Agent works again in Notion AI, the AI keeps going after plans, and task checklists show real progress.

### 🛠️ Notion Start Agent fixed

- **Startup:** the command list sends itself instead of staying in the chat box, even when Notion reformats it.
- **New Notion layout:** the /ai page, chat threads and New chat button are recognised again.
- **Sends recognised:** emoji, curly quotes and formatting changes no longer make commands look unsent.
- **Startup loop fixed:** a short extra line after “PlazCode is ready.” is accepted.

### 🧠 Plans and tasks

- **Keeps going:** in Plan Mode and Ultracode the AI starts building after writing its plan.
- **Working checklist:** numbered steps from your request and the AI’s plan, with progress.
- **Clean results:** skill notes are sent once per task instead of replacing command results.

### ⚙️ Tools

- **Addons:** the AI is told when Blender is connected.
- **Prompt enhancer:** only runs before creation saves and never blocks a command.

### 🧪 Checks

- Platform/browser regressions passed. Start Agent was confirmed on a live Notion account; the later fixes were tested automatically only.

### 📥 How to update

- Open PlazCode, or choose **Updates → Update now**.
- Manual downloads: **PlazCode-1.24.10.zip** for Windows, **PlazCode-macOS-1.24.10.zip** for Mac, or **PlazCode-Firefox-1.24.10.zip** for Firefox.
- Reload your extension and refresh your AI tabs. Firefox users: reload the temporary add-on after restarting Firefox.
- Your settings, memory and connections are kept.

Test details are included in VALIDATION.txt.
