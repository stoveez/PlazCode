## ✨ PlazCode 1.24.3

Less wasted work, a smaller Windows download and a refreshed workspace.

### ⚡ Smoother performance

- **Less lag:** unchanged tools and logs stay in place. New log lines append without rebuilding the list.
- **Less background work:** fewer repeated requests and refreshes while hidden.

### 📦 Smaller downloads

- **Windows:** no longer bundles the Mac app and Mac memory helpers. Mac keeps its own complete download. Features and starter skills are preserved.

### 📁 Better workspace

- **Explorer:** a cleaner game tree, class icons, search, and script/property panels that match your theme.
- **Navigation:** centered collapsed icons and your requested page order. Updates is in Settings.

### 🧠 See your effort level

- **Desktop and bar:** show your selected effort. Low starts yellow, Max is red, and Ultracode is purple.
- **Ultracode:** selected pages get a purple outline while keeping your theme glow, with a smooth transition.

### 🔧 Startup and chat fixes

- **Windows startup:** skips unnecessary process scans and detects old agents across display languages.
- **Startup:** another verified text-send path when Notion file uploads fail.
- **Normal chat:** idle or disconnected chats no longer turn ordinary messages into queued follow-ups.
- **Faster reply detection:** recognize finished responses in more languages, removing avoidable waiting after Notion finishes.
- **Engine selection:** a delayed settings load no longer overrides your choice of AgentScript.

### 📥 How to update

- Open PlazCode, or choose **Updates → Update now**.
- Manual downloads: **PlazCode-1.24.3.zip** for Windows, **PlazCode-macOS-1.24.3.zip** for Mac, or **PlazCode-Firefox-1.24.3.zip** for Firefox.
- Reload your extension and refresh your AI tabs. Firefox users: reload the temporary add-on after restarting Firefox.
- Your settings, memory and connections are kept.

Test details are included in VALIDATION.txt.
