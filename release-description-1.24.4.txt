## ✨ PlazCode 1.24.4

Less repeated work, easier Settings and fixes for Notion and screenshots.

### ⚡ Faster Ultracode

- **Less waiting:** a 62% smaller instruction pack, less repeated planning, and reuse of verified information.
- **Careful problem solving:** keeps its skills, safety checks and verification.

### 🖥️ Easier workspace

- **Scrolling:** reach the bottom of pages, with scrollbars that match your theme.
- **Settings:** click a section to open or close it. Updates is back in the sidebar.
- **Effort labels:** the whole label matches the level’s color. The bar says Ultracode; Settings keeps Ultracode (Beta).

### 🔧 Chat and screenshot fixes

- **Notion:** better send-button detection, including tall composers and delayed startup controls. Large tool replies have a verified text-send fallback.
- **Screenshots:** Studio captures use the right bridge. ChatGPT waits for an attachment and sends it once; failed uploads show an error.

### 📥 How to update

- Open PlazCode, or choose **Updates → Update now**.
- Manual downloads: **PlazCode-1.24.4.zip** for Windows, **PlazCode-macOS-1.24.4.zip** for Mac, or **PlazCode-Firefox-1.24.4.zip** for Firefox.
- Reload your extension and refresh your AI tabs. Firefox users: reload the temporary add-on after restarting Firefox.
- Your settings, memory and connections are kept.
