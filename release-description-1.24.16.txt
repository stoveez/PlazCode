## ✨ PlazCode 1.24.16

ClickUp startup receipt and model-refusal fixes.

### 🛠️ Fixes

- Accepted startup parts continue after rendered text changes or delayed acknowledgements.
- Compact defaults reduce startup context while preserving custom instructions.
- Explicit model refusals stop without repeated handshake requests.

### 🧪 Validation and limitations

- Windows, macOS, Linux and Chromium/Firefox checks; packaged Mac desktop lifetime checked before publication.
- Affected live accounts were not reproduced. A ClickUp model can still decline. Windows unsigned; Mac not notarized.

### 📥 How to update

- Open PlazCode, or choose **Updates → Update now**.
- Manual downloads: **PlazCode-1.24.16.zip** for Windows, **PlazCode-macOS-1.24.16.zip** for Mac, or **PlazCode-Firefox-1.24.16.zip** for Firefox.
- Reload your extension and refresh your AI tabs. Firefox users: reload the temporary add-on after restarting Firefox.
- Your settings, memory and connections are kept.

Test details are included in VALIDATION.txt.
